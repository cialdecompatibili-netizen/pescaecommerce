To choose your first rod, what matters most is where and how you want to fish.

## Spinning

It suits lure fishing, from shore or boat, and is versatile for beginners.

## Telescopic

It packs into little space: handy for travel, lake and river.

## Surfcasting

It is made for long casts from the beach and needs a suitable reel.

## Before buying

Think about the kind of fishing, your budget and local rules. If in doubt, write to us.
