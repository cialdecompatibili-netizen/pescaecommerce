Pesca Ecommerce is online: a shop for fishing gear for sea, lake and river.

## What you will find

Six ranges: rods, reels, lines and braids, hooks and terminal tackle, baits and lures, accessories. Each page has a clear description.

## What is coming

On the blog we publish guides to choosing your gear. If you have a request, write to us from the Contact page.
