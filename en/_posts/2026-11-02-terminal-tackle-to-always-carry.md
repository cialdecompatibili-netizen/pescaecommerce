A small stock of terminal tackle saves you from cutting your fishing day short.

- **Hooks** in several sizes.
- **Swivels and snaps** to change lures quickly.
- **Sinkers** of different weights.
- **Spare line**, nylon or fluorocarbon.
- **A pair of pliers** to unhook and cut line.

Keep everything in a box with compartments so you can always find it.
